## The rule (`lib/htfEntryRule.js`, pure; `lib/htfEntryLive.js`, live wiring)
- **Direction** (per symbol, on every 1h close): LONG when EMA21 > EMA200 on BOTH 4h and 1D and price is above EMA21(4h); SHORT mirrored; else NONE. Publish `htf.direction`, `htf.since`, the 1h swing low/high (last confirmed pivot, `lib/geometry.js`), the 1h measured target (last 1h impulse projected from the swing), `room` on 1h.
- **Trigger**: a 1m/3m/5m flag from the existing detector reaching `triggering` in the HTF direction, while `htf.direction` is set.
- **Plan**: entry = the trigger close; stop = 1h swing low (long) / high (short) minus/plus 0.1 × ATR(1h), floored by NF (`max(0.5 × ATR15m, 3 × cost)`) and capped by the 3 % scalp guard; TP1 = 1h measured target; require TP1 ≥ 2.5R off that stop and net R:R ≥ 1.0; TP2 = next 1h/4h zone if any.
- **Management**: the existing +1R auto-trail applies (do NOT exempt it); EXIT alerts: `EXIT · structure` (1h close beyond the swing that defined the stop, in the wrong direction) and `EXIT · time` (72 h). One trigger per symbol per HTF regime per 4 h (cooldown), max one open HTF trade per symbol (executor cap still binds).
