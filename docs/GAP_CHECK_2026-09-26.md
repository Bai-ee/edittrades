# Gap check — 2026-09-26 (T-11, Agent F3, read-only)

Owner question: replay of the live rules scores ~5 GOOD/day (F2 frequency study); the live
tracker (10-min cron since 2026-09-24) counted 4 GOOD in ~3 days (~1.3/day). Which is it,
and why. **Both readings are real** — they measure different things over a window where
the config itself also moved. No config/engine/rule change made; no deploy; no orders.

## Method

- **G1**: replayed `V0` (confirmed by the F2 study to be byte-identical to its `L0`,
  i.e. the on-disk config, `configVersion 2026.09.24-5`, unmodified) over the tracker's own
  window, 2026-09-24T00:00Z → 2026-09-26T16:08Z (64.13h), `--step 1`. History: the existing
  `deep60-2026-09-24` fixture (ends 14:33Z 9/24) merged with a fresh Kraken capture +
  `--backfill-1m 2400` (`test/fixtures/history/gap-2026-09-26/`, 1m from 12:10Z 9/24 through
  now) — merged into `test/fixtures/history/merged-2026-09-26/` (dedup by timestamp, gap
  capture wins on overlap). `scripts/replay-rules.js`'s own coverage/frequency stats read
  `manifest.json`'s full span (87.6 days), not `--from/--to`, so those printed rollups are
  not usable here — the per-call output (`var/gap-check/V0.calls.jsonl`, left untracked, is
  the source of truth: **26 GOOD first-ready events** in the window (9.7/day, well above
  both the F2 baseline and the tracker's count).
- **G2**: `edittrades-tracker` `origin/main` `data/outcomes.jsonl` + `data/calls/*.jsonl`:
  **5** GOOD rec calls in the window, all with a matching raw capture row (no drop by
  `collect.js`/`aggregate.js` — `extractCalls`'s dedup-by-signature never discarded a
  captured GOOD here). Capture cadence: 440 captures in the window, ~10 min apart (one
  121-min gap).
- **G3/G4**: `data/telegram-alerts/` (the cron's own per-minute sent-alert log; only
  2026-09-25/26 exist, no 2026-09-24 file) lists **12** distinct `GOOD`-kind alerts and
  **11** `GOOD_ENDED` in that 2-day slice — matching 12 of the replay's 16 events for the
  same sub-window exactly (symbol/entry/stop/tp1 all equal), which also rules out a
  meaningful data/candle difference (class e: not observed).
- Config check: `configVersion` inside the tracker's own window changed **5 times**:
  `2026.09.23-7` (`minRR 3.0`) → `-1` (13:12Z) → `-3` (18:47Z, `minNetRR 2.0` on) → `-4`
  (19:29Z) → `-5` (21:07Z, `minRR 2.5`, net gate off — the config the replay used
  throughout). The stricter config was live for the **first 21h07m of the 64h13m window**
  (33%).

The 5 tracker-captured rows are exactly the tracker's own 5 GOOD calls (cross-check

`GOOD_ENDED` at 22:29:44, no `GOOD` in between — replay's own ready close is 22:28:00,
a single 1-minute candle).

## Single largest cause

**Capture cadence vs. GOOD-window duration.** Restricted to the config-matched sub-window
(17 events, no config confound), the tracker still only captured 5/17 (29%); all 12 misses
there are cadence, not config. GOOD windows run ~2–5 minutes median (Telegram's own
GOOD→GOOD_ENDED gaps) and sometimes under 1 minute — shorter than even the 1-minute
Telegram cron reliably catches, and far shorter than the tracker's 10-minute poll. The
config-version mismatch (33% of the window ran a stricter, evolving rule than the replay
assumed) is real and explains the 9/24-morning gap specifically, but is a one-time
rollout-day artifact, not the steady-state driver.

## Fix (described, not implemented)

1. Poll `/api/scalp-context` every 1 minute instead of every 10 (matches the Telegram
   cron's own cadence; would have caught ~12/17 more in this window alone).
2. Or: ingest the Telegram cron's own per-minute `GOOD`/`GOOD_ENDED` sent-alert log
   (`data/telegram-alerts/`) as a second, denser source of scored GOOD calls, since it
   already runs every minute and already writes entry/stop/tp1/candidateId.
3. A served-call-based count (already partially in place, `source: 'served'`) only helps
   when the GPT happens to be queried inside the window; it is not a systematic fix.

## Summary

- Reconciliation: 26 replay GOOD events → 5 tracker-captured, 12 cadence-miss (9

- Single largest cause: 10-minute capture cadence against 2–5 (sometimes <1) minute GOOD
  windows — 100% of misses in the config-matched sub-window are this, not config.
- Proposed fix: 1-minute capture cadence, or ingest the Telegram cron's per-minute
  GOOD/GOOD_ENDED log directly (both described only, not implemented).
