Bitcoin Economic Value

Bitcoin’s price against its long-term economic anchors. A reference, not a claim about what Bitcoin is worth.

What goes into it

Three structurally independent anchors: aggregate holder cost basis (realized price), long-term market structure (the 200-week moving average) and estimated production cost (mining economics). They are blended in log space, and the weighting renormalises over whichever anchors have data on a given date.
