Wallet & risk policy

Advanced — fees, slippage & funding

A planned stop is not a guaranteed maximum loss. Fees are charged on entry and exit, slippage applies at the stop, and funding accrues while the position is held. These default to zero so the headline risk figure stays the clean planned number; apply a preset to see cost-adjusted output.

Storage & backup
What these terms mean
Risk per trade
The percentage of your trading wallet lost if the planned stop executes.
Exposure
The total market value the position controls. A $10,000 position is not $10,000 of risk.
Leverage
Reduces the margin required. It does not make the underlying trade less risky — it moves the liquidation price closer.
Open risk
Estimated loss if every open position reaches its planned stop at once.
Est. liquidation
An approximation for an isolated position. Never use it as a stop-loss.
