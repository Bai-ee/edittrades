    How this works

    Levels

    The ladder runs −2 to 5. Level 0 is where everyone starts and is anchored to the default risk policy. Everything above 0 is earned over many closed trades; everything below is defensive.

    Slow up, fast down. A promotion takes a full window of trades with every gate clear, and only one level at a time. A drawdown can drop several levels in a single evaluation, with no grace period. A good run never raises risk on its own — it earns levels, and the level is what widens the envelope.

    Not trading is not the same as trading badly. A gap in activity never demotes; it pauses promotion and applies a conservatism haircut for the first few trades back.

    Where the numbers come from

    Every figure on this page — size, leverage, max loss, level, progress, open risk, drawdown — is produced by the shared risk engine. This page formats and displays; it calculates nothing of its own, and no AI is involved in any number.

    On-chain data is authoritative. A screenshot or a manual correction never silently overrides something the chain observed directly: the disagreement is shown on the record for you to resolve.

    If the wallet cannot be read, the page says wallet data unavailable rather than showing a balance. A stale read is reported as stale and the recommendation stops presenting itself as confident.

    Storage & privacy

    Wallet address, snapshots, classified transfers, level history, strategy versions and your trade corrections are stored in this browser only. None of it is uploaded, and none of it is written to the project’s shared database — that database is world-readable, and account size does not belong in it.

    Clearing site data clears all of it. There is no server copy.

    Decision support only. Every output describes risk capacity — what this account can currently carry — and never where a price is going. A recommendation is not a prediction and REDUCE does not mean a trade is wrong.
